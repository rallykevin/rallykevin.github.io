<h1 id="about-me"></h1>


I am a Ph.D candidate studying at Security Optimization Lab (<a href="http://sor.snu.ac.kr/">SORLab</a>) in Seoul National University, advised by professor  <a href="http://sor.snu.ac.kr/document/professors">Yunheung Paek</a>.

My current research topics lie at <strong>Privacy-Preserving (Enhancing) Computing</strong> and <strong>Domain-Specific Acceleration</strong>, focusing on performance and accuracy enhancements of when adapting <strong>Fully Homomorphic Encryption</strong> to specific applications such as MLaaS. However, my research interests are not bound to them and include broader fields including <strong>Trusted Execution Environment, Multi-Party Computing, Artificial Intelligence,</strong> and <strong>Post-Quantum Cryptography</strong>.

You can find more information about me from my <a href="https://rallykevin.github.io/assets/files/kvnamcv.pdf">CV</a> or by navigating this webpage including <a href="https://rallykevin.github.io/publications/">Publications</a>. If you are interested or have questions about my research, please feel free to contact me.
