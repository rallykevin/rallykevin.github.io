<h1 id="about-me"></h1>


I am a Ph.D candidate studying at Security Optimization Lab (<a href="http://sor.snu.ac.kr/">SORLab</a>) in Seoul National University, advised by professor  <a href="http://sor.snu.ac.kr/document/professors">Yunheung Paek</a>.

My current research topics are <strong>Privacy Enhancing Computing Systems</strong> as well as <strong>Hardware/System/AI Security</strong>, focusing on performance and accuracy enhancements of when adapting <strong>Fully Homomorphic Encryption</strong> to specific applications such as MLaaS. I have also participated in research related to <strong>Trusted Execution Environment, Multi-Party Computing, Artificial Intelligence,</strong> and <strong>Post-Quantum Cryptography</strong>.

<strong style="color:#0729b1"> I am graduating in Feb. 2026, and am looking for a position where I can continue my research career in academia.</strong>

More information about me can be found in my <a href="https://rallykevin.github.io/assets/files/kvnamcv.pdf">Curriculum Vitae</a>. You can also navigate pages of this webpage including <a href="https://rallykevin.github.io/publications/">Publications</a>. If you are interested or have questions about my research, please feel free to contact me.
